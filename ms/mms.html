Building Blocks of a Model

Decision Variables
x₁, x₂, ... — choices under control. "How many units?"
Parameters
Fixed inputs: costs, demands, capacities. Data from reality.
Objective Function
Maximize profit or minimize cost — the goal we optimize.
Constraints
Limits: budgets, resources, time. The boundaries of possibility.
Feasible Region
All decision combinations satisfying constraints.
Optimal Solution
The point inside feasible region maximizing/minimizing objective.
