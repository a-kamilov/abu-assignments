Course Overview

Analytical Methods & Decision Science

Management Science focuses on using analytical methods, mathematical models, optimization techniques, and structured decision-making processes to solve complex problems. Throughout this course, topics included mathematical modeling, linear programming, branch-and-bound methods, binary optimization, and transportation networks.

Each project translates theoretical concepts into interactive demonstrations — showing how constraints shape feasible regions, how search algorithms prune branches, and how to maximize satisfaction under budget limits. The portfolio integrates evidence-based learning with hands-on exploration.

Final Reflection

Insights & Intellectual Growth

⭐ Most Interesting Project

Branch & Bound Explorer — This project was the most interesting because it demonstrated how optimization algorithms intelligently eliminate large portions of the search space instead of checking every possible solution.

⚡ Most Challenging Project

Feasible Region Explorer — Understanding how multiple constraints interact and how small changes affect feasible regions required careful analysis and interpretation.

🎯 Most Valuable Project

Pizza Optimizer Lab — This project showed how mathematical optimization can support practical decision-making by balancing objectives and limitations.

🔄 How My Understanding Changed

At the beginning of the course, optimization appeared to be mainly equations and calculations. Through these projects, I learned that Management Science is fundamentally about structuring decisions, analyzing alternatives, and improving outcomes through systematic reasoning.
