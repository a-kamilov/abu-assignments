The gravity model
Gravity Model of Trade
Tij = A × (Yi × Yj) / Dij
Tij — Trade volume Yi, Yj — GDP Dij — Distance

Larger economies trade more

Higher GDP means more production & consumption → larger trade volumes.

Distance reduces trade

Higher transport costs & longer lead times reduce trade.

Trade barriers matter

Borders & regulations act like extra distance.
