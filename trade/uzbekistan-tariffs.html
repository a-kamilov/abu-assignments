01

Project Overview

Tariffs are taxes on imported goods. While they can protect local industries, in Uzbekistan extreme tariffs on cars, gold, and medicine create unusual market distortions. This project analyzes these policies and their consumer impact.

💸 How Tariffs Affect Prices

Tariffs are added directly to the cost of imported goods. Importers pass these costs to consumers, making products far more expensive than without the tax.

🛒 Effect on Consumers

Consumers face fewer imported choices and higher prices. Imported cars become 2–3× more expensive; medicines see ~22% increases.

🏭 Protecting Local Industry

High tariffs shield domestic producers like UzAuto Motors, supporting local employment but limiting competition.

⚖️ Trade-offs & Debates

Protectionism creates winners (local producers) and losers (consumers). Essential goods taxation raises ethical questions.

Foreign Imported Cars

Extreme customs duties make imported vehicles 2–3× more expensive

🏭 Protecting Domestic Industry

Uzbekistan imposes some of the highest car import tariffs in Central Asia. Combined duties, excise, VAT, and fees push final prices to 2–3 times the original MSRP. This protects UzAuto Motors but limits consumer choice.

Gold & Jewelry Import Restrictions

Strict government control over gold trade

🚫 State Control Policy

Uzbekistan restricts gold imports beyond personal allowances. Travelers face declaration requirements and potential taxes. The policy protects national wealth but burdens citizens returning with legally purchased jewelry.

What Did I Learn?

"Tariffs are not just numbers on a government document — they are real decisions that change whether families can afford a car, whether a patient can access medicine."

📖 What I Learned

Tariffs are strategic policy tools — sometimes protecting infant industries, sometimes generating revenue. Uzbekistan shows all three motivations.

👨‍👩‍👧 Impact on People

Families pay double for cars; patients face higher medicine costs. Tariffs translate policy into daily hardship.

🛠️ Skills Gained

Research, data analysis, critical thinking, and translating tariff schedules into understandable examples.

💭 Personal Reflections

Taxing essential medicine prioritizes revenue over health — made me question whether all tariffs are equal.
